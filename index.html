Uncertainty-Aware Multimodal Inference under Sensor Degradation

A controlled multimodal state-estimation framework with three heterogeneous sensors under a taxonomy of degradation and distribution-shift regimes (noise, bias, dropout, correlated multi-sensor failure, sensor spoofing).

Read more
  • Built a five-method baseline ladder (fixed WLS, a strong non-learned adaptive heuristic, learned fusion, the proposed learned-uncertainty model, and an oracle ceiling) under a 5-seed reproducibility protocol; an uncertainty-shuffling ablation confirmed gains stem from genuine reliability information rather than added model capacity.
  • Showed predicted uncertainty tracks true estimation error (Pearson r=0.73, Spearman rho=0.74) and used a NEES-based calibration analysis (the classical Kalman-filter consistency check) to show shift-induced miscalibration is a noise-shape mismatch rather than a scale effect, independently confirmed via failed post-hoc recalibration and failed classical robust (Huber/Tukey) refitting.
  • Identified a reproducible 13% RMSE improvement over the strongest non-learned baseline specifically under sensor-reliability-signal corruption and spoofing, and validated all core findings across two independent implementations (hand-derived NumPy backpropagation and PyTorch autograd).
  • Python
  • PyTorch

Mixed-Integer Optimization for EV Charging

Scheduling electric-vehicle charging under charger, time-window, and demand constraints as a resource-constrained optimization problem.

Read more
  • Formulated electric-vehicle charging allocation as a mixed-integer optimisation problem (resource-constrained scheduling) with charger, time-window, and user-demand constraints; solved to global optimality using the open-source HiGHS solver, scaling to 100+ vehicles in under 0.1s.
  • Analysed trade-offs between energy cost, charging demand, and user waiting time, showing how resource availability (charger count) reshapes the achievable cost--waiting Pareto frontier.
  • Extended the deterministic formulation to a two-stage stochastic optimisation framework capturing uncertainty in vehicle arrival times and energy requirements; validated via out-of-sample testing that the stochastic policy outperforms a deterministic point-forecast policy (positive Value of the Stochastic Solution).
  • Implemented the full framework in Python and evaluated nominal and uncertainty-aware solutions through computational experiments (scalability, cost--waiting trade-offs, out-of-sample policy comparison), producing reproducible results and visualisations.
  • Python
  • Optimization
