Engineering Degree in Data Science & Information Systems, and M.Sc. in Complex Systems Engineering (Signal, Vision & Control), University of Toulon

Lately I have been eavesdropping on marine mammals in underwater recordings and keeping an eye on ships from satellite data. Turns out I just like building things that sense, process and respond.

My current obsession is teaching models to say "I'm not sure" when their sensors go bad, instead of confidently guessing. Long term, I want systems you can trust outside the lab, where sensors drift, break and sometimes just have a bad day.

A toy illustration, not my research method: a Kalman filter in which each sensor's weight depends on how well it agrees with the median of the three. It copes when one sensor fails; with two failing there is no majority left to catch the fault.

Research interests

  • Uncertainty-aware multimodal inference: combining heterogeneous sensors reliably under noise and degradation.
  • Statistical signal processing: detection and classification in noisy, real-world signals such as passive acoustics.
  • State estimation and data fusion: reconstructing knowledge from heterogeneous sources.
  • Deep learning for vision and remote sensing: segmentation, multimodal retrieval and learning under evolving data.
  • Optimization under uncertainty: scheduling and decision problems with variable demand and constraints.
