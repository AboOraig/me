    Uncertainty-Aware Multimodal Inference under Sensor Degradation

    A controlled synthetic study of multimodal state estimation from three heterogeneous sensors under noise, bias faults, dropout, heavy-tailed noise, correlated multi-sensor failure, quality-signal corruption and deceptive sensors.

    Details
    • Built a five-method baseline ladder (fixed WLS, a non-learned adaptive heuristic that uses the sensors' self-reported quality, learned fusion, a learned per-sensor uncertainty model, and an oracle-variance reference) under a 5-seed protocol with fixed evaluation sets. The learned uncertainty model is a near-tie with the heuristic (mean RMSE 0.705 vs 0.701), and a variance-shuffling ablation shows its predicted variances carry real information (uncertainty–error correlation Pearson r = 0.73, Spearman ρ = 0.74).
    • Tested two attempts to beat the heuristic, a cross-sensor residual feature and Huber/Tukey robust reweighting, and reported both as negative results.
    • Under severe corruption of the quality signal the learned model is less affected than the heuristic (RMSE 1.153 vs 1.328, about 13% lower), but fixed weighting is better than both (0.838); the explanation I propose (bounded predicted variance) is untested.
    • Showed with a NEES-based calibration analysis that calibration error rises about fivefold under variance-matched heavy-tailed noise (ECE 0.025 to 0.129) while predicted sharpness is unchanged, and that one moment-matched scalar recalibration does not fix it, consistent with a tail-shape mismatch.
    • NumPy implementation with hand-derived backpropagation, checked against finite differences, plus a PyTorch cross-check of the core model; code and a tagged release are public.
    • Python
    • PyTorch

  • Learned Regularization for Robust Image Reconstruction

    Formulated sparse-view CT reconstruction as an inverse problem under noise, incomplete angular sampling, and forward-model uncertainty; built a differentiable sparse-view Radon operator with exact adjoint.

    Details
    • Integrated a noise-conditioned CNN denoiser as a learned prior into a Plug-and-Play proximal-gradient reconstruction framework, benchmarked against FBP, Tikhonov, and TV regularization with validation-tuned hyperparameters for every method.
    • Evaluated reconstruction accuracy under varying noise levels, angular undersampling, and train-test distribution shifts using PSNR/SSIM with statistical testing, characterizing the trade-off between the learned prior's in-distribution expressiveness and its stability under distribution shift and forward-model mismatch.
    • Showed the learned prior improves PSNR by up to 4 dB over TV under high noise or few projection angles (paired Wilcoxon, p<0.001), but underperforms TV in the near-noiseless regime, and identified that its advantage narrows under forward-model mismatch and reverses under distribution shift, where it hallucinates in-distribution structure on out-of-domain inputs.
    • Python
    • PyTorch
    • scikit-image
    • NumPy/SciPy
    • Pandas
    • Matplotlib

  • Mixed-Integer Optimization for EV Charging

    Scheduling electric-vehicle charging under charger, time-window, and demand constraints as a resource-constrained optimization problem.

    Details
    • Formulated electric-vehicle charging allocation as a mixed-integer optimization problem (resource-constrained scheduling) with charger, time-window, and user-demand constraints; solved to global optimality using the open-source HiGHS solver, scaling to 100+ vehicles in under 0.1s.
    • Analyzed trade-offs between energy cost, charging demand, and user waiting time, showing how resource availability (charger count) reshapes the achievable cost–waiting Pareto frontier.
    • Extended the deterministic formulation to a two-stage stochastic optimization framework capturing uncertainty in vehicle arrival times and energy requirements; validated via out-of-sample testing that the stochastic policy outperforms a deterministic point-forecast policy (positive Value of the Stochastic Solution).
    • Implemented the full framework in Python and evaluated nominal and uncertainty-aware solutions through computational experiments (scalability, cost–waiting trade-offs, out-of-sample policy comparison), producing reproducible results and visualizations.
    • Python
    • Optimization
    • Mixed-Integer Programming
